# Reflection

During this laboratory activity, I learned more about the three major cloud computing platforms: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Among the three platforms, AWS impressed me the most because of the wide range of services it offers for different types of organizations. I learned that AWS can support small startup companies, large businesses, and even global organizations. However, this activity also helped me understand that the most popular cloud platform is not always the best choice. The right platform should depend on the specific needs and requirements of the organization.

I discovered that AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three platforms provide important services for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and identity management. They also allow organizations to access cloud resources without having to purchase and maintain all their own physical servers. However, each provider has its own strengths. Azure is a good choice for organizations that already use Microsoft technologies, while GCP is known for its strengths in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes.

Business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform. Organizations should consider factors such as their budget, existing technologies, security requirements, scalability, and future plans. For example, a university that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may benefit from using Microsoft Azure. On the other hand, an AI research company may choose Google Cloud because of its strong AI and machine learning services. A startup or global company may consider AWS because of its flexible and scalable infrastructure.

By acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that understanding the client's needs is important before recommending any technology. I improved my skills in researching cloud platforms, comparing services, and making recommendations based on different business scenarios. This activity also helped me understand how Linux servers can be migrated to cloud virtual machines.

After completing this mission, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio became more organized and complete because I added research files, comparison tables, client recommendations, reflections, and screenshots. Overall, this activity improved my knowledge of cloud computing and helped me develop my skills in making better technology decisions.
